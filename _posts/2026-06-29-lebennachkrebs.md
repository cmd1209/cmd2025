“Das Leben nach Krebs, Life After Cancer" was created as a focused WordPress landing page for an event format dedicated to life after cancer therapy. The project needed to communicate a sensitive and important topic clearly, warmly and professionally — with space for program information, expert voices, partner visibility and a clear path to registration.

{% include post-gallery.html %}

The implementation combined visual design, WordPress development and responsive fine-tuning. Custom sections, speaker portraits, sponsor and partner elements, event information, CTA areas and detailed styling were built into a flexible page structure that could be updated as the event developed.
