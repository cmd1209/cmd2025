The Nexxt Level Blog was redesigned and rebuilt to create a clearer digital platform for women’s health topics, events, sponsors and editorial content. The project focused on turning an organically grown WordPress site into a more structured, flexible and visually consistent experience that could support both current content and future expansion.

{% include post-gallery.html %}

The implementation included a new staging workflow, revised navigation, theme overview pages, custom CSS, responsive adjustments, sponsor sections, blog structures and reusable design patterns. Special attention was given to making the site easier to maintain over time, while keeping the visual language bold, accessible and aligned with the Nexxt Level brand.
